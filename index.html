Radiance Plus exists because good educators deserve good tools — not just to teach well, but to be seen, supported and paid what their work is worth.

We sit between consulting, coaching and training: close enough to a classroom to know what's practical, and structured enough to help a school or an individual educator build something that lasts.

Whether we're rebuilding a school's brand from the ground up or coaching a single teacher toward the next stage of their career, the goal is the same — help education professionals lead with confidence.
