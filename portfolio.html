What She Does

Bringing emotional intelligence into everyday teaching.

Ugofunmi's work with Radiance Plus focuses on the human side of education — how educators show up, connect, and lead.

SEL-Focused Teacher Coaching

One-on-one coaching that helps educators build self-awareness, regulate stress, and bring more presence into the classroom.

Content for Educators

Ongoing content and commentary that helps teachers see themselves — and their growth — reflected honestly online.

Classroom Culture Workshops

Practical sessions on building classrooms where students feel safe, seen, and able to engage.

Teens & Children Mentoring

Structured mentoring that helps young people build confidence, character and emotional vocabulary.
